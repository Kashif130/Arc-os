✨ Create & Deploy NFT Collection
ℹ️ This will deploy a full ERC-721 contract on Arc Testnet for your NFT collection.
📋 How It Works
Step 1 — Fill in your collection details
Step 2 — Upload your artwork to IPFS (use nft.storage or Pinata)
Step 3 — Enter the IPFS base URI for your metadata
Step 4 — Click Deploy — MetaMask will prompt for confirmation
Step 5 — After deploy, go to Mint tab to mint tokens!
📊 Metadata Format
{
  "name": "Token #1",
  "description": "...",
  "image": "ipfs://...",
  "attributes": [...]
}
Each token ID maps to baseURI + tokenId + ".json"

ℹ️ Instructions
• Deploy an ERC-721 contract first (use Create tab)
• Enter the deployed contract address
• Set the token ID you want to mint
• Optionally enter a recipient address
• Click Mint — MetaMask will ask for confirmation

Tip: Each token ID must be unique per contract.

🏛️ Real World Assets (RWA) Kya Hai?

RWA (Real World Assets) wo physical ya traditional financial assets hain jo blockchain par tokenize kiye jaate hain — jaise property, government bonds, stocks, gold, aur invoices.

Arc + RWA: Arc ka programmable money concept RWA ke liye perfect hai kyunki:

📜 Smart contracts automatically payments distribute karte hain (rent, dividends)
🔒 USDC mein settle — no currency risk
🌐 Global investors 24/7 access kar sakte hain
💎 Fractionalization — ₹1 crore property mein 1000 log invest kar sakte hain
📊 Asset Types Supported
🏠 Real Estate
Commercial/residential properties. Rent income automatically distribute hoti hai USDC mein via Arc smart contracts.
📜 Government Bonds
T-bills, sovereign bonds. Coupon payments programmable hain — maturity par auto-redeem.
📈 Equity / Stocks
Company shares on-chain. Dividend distributions via Arc's subscription-like payment rails.
🪙 Commodities
Gold, silver, oil — tokenized. Price oracles se real-time valuation update hoti hai.
